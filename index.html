Rows Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026 App: Rows Contact: noahtodd07@icloud.com

In one sentence: Rows keeps everything on your device — it has no servers, no account system, and no analytics, so there is nothing for us to collect.

Rows reads Apple Health data (with your permission) to automatically check off habits, and it never sends that data, or anything else about you, anywhere. Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple.

1. Information we collect 2. Apple Health data 3. Subscriptions & purchases 4. Where your data lives 5. Third parties 6. Your choices & deletion 7. Children's privacy 8. Changes to this policy 9. Contact

01Information we collect

Rows does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Every habit, log entry, streak, and setting you create is stored only in the app's local storage on your device (and shared on-device with the Rows widget via an App Group container, so your widget can show the same data). None of it is sent to us or to any third party, because Rows has no backend server to send it to.

Rows sends local notifications (reminders, streak nudges) that are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. It does not use push notifications, so no notification data passes through any server, ours or Apple's.

02Apple Health data

If you grant permission, Rows reads step count, sleep, workout, and other activity data from Apple Health so it can automatically mark linked habits as complete. This access is read-only in normal use.

Data read
Steps, sleep, workouts, and related activity metrics you choose to link to a habit
Data written
None — Rows does not write anything to Apple Health
Where it goes
Nowhere. It's read on-device, used to update your habit log on-device, and never transmitted
Used for advertising?
No, never

You can review or revoke Rows' Health access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Rows on your device.

03Subscriptions & purchases

Rows Premium is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly) processed entirely through Apple's App Store billing (StoreKit). Rows checks your subscription status on-device using Apple's own transaction records — we never see or store your payment details, billing address, or card information; Apple handles all of that under its own privacy policy.

Your subscription automatically renews for the same length and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.

04Where your data lives

All app data — habits, logs, streaks, preferences — is stored locally using on-device storage, plus a local App Group container so the Rows widget can read the same data without a network round-trip. There is no cloud sync and no account to log into, so there is no copy of your data outside your own device(s).

Deleting the Rows app deletes this data along with it.

05Third parties

Rows contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs). It makes no network requests at all. We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties, because none passes through our hands in the first place.

06Your choices & deletion

  • Revoke Health access anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Turn off notifications anytime in iOS Settings → Notifications → Rows.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app — since nothing is stored on a server, this removes all Rows data completely and immediately. There is no account to separately request deletion from.

07Children's privacy

Rows is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in the Kids Category. Because Rows collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

08Changes to this policy

If Rows' data practices change — for example, if a future version adds sync or an account — this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, before that change ships.

09Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to noahtodd07@icloud.com.
